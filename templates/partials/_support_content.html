How to reach us when something isn't working.

Contact us

support@synzo.ai
We respond within 2 business days.

What to include in your report

To help us resolve issues quickly, please include:

  • Your organization ID (visible at /dashboard).
  • The tool name (summarize_document, translate_document, redact_pii, analyze_image, or detect_faces).
  • Approximate timestamp of the failed call.
  • The error code if you saw one (-32001 through -32005).
  • What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened.
Do not paste document contents into support emails. We don't need to see your file to debug — the metadata above is enough, and pasting contents into email creates an unnecessary copy of your data.

Try self-serve first

Many common issues are covered in the Troubleshooting section of our docs. If you're hitting a specific error code, that section has a one-line fix for each.

Reporting a security vulnerability

Security issues use a separate channel — please do not send them to the general support mailbox. Email security@synzo.ai, or see our security disclosure page for the full process.
